ABOUT NOVLIRI

Find the right software
without the noise.

NOVLIRI is a software discovery directory built to make finding useful digital tools simpler.

Whether you're a student, creator, developer, professional, entrepreneur, or simply looking for a better way to get something done, NOVLIRI helps you discover software based on what you actually need.

Built around your needs.

Instead of forcing you to search through endless lists, we organize software around practical needs such as education, business, productivity, artificial intelligence, marketing, design, communication, and software development.

Simple software discovery.

Each listing gives you a straightforward explanation of what a tool does so you can quickly decide whether it's worth exploring. Our directory will continue to expand as new and useful software is discovered.

Our goal

Make discovering useful software faster, simpler, and easier to understand.
